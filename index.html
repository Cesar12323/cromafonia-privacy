Cromafonia – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

In short: Cromafonia has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone. Some optional features contact third-party services directly from your phone, only when you use them, and send only what is needed to answer you.

Who we are

Cromafonia (package it.cromafonia) is developed by Doctor Ai, who is responsible for this app. Contact: doctoresai0@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

The YouTube search feature uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. Videos open in the YouTube app; Cromafonia does not download YouTube content or play it in the background. You can stop this access at any time by removing your key (Settings → Remove YouTube); you can also manage or revoke your key in the Google Cloud Console.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or analyse your data.
  • We use no ads, trackers, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

Permissions

  • Music and audio: to find and play the songs saved on your phone.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep music playing in the background, with a playback notification and controls.
  • Internet: only for the optional features listed above.

AI-generated content

Answers from the AI DJ, translations and lyrics explanations are generated automatically by the model you choose and may contain mistakes. You can report an inappropriate answer with the "Report" button: the report is sent with the app you choose (for example your email app) to doctoresai0@gmail.com and contains only what you see before sending.

Children

Cromafonia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf: everything is on your phone and you control it. For data sent to third-party services, their policies apply. For any question write to doctoresai0@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect.
